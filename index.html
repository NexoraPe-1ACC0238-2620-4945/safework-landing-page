BEFORE YOU START
  1. Install manually

    Once the APK is published, download it and allow installation from the source you use to open it. Google Play distribution is not available.

  2. Request your company invitation

    An authorized person issues an invitation linked to your email and company. Invitations are shared by your company; automatic email delivery is not enabled.

  3. Create your account and log in

    Register as WORKER with the invitation. EMPLOYER access is granted administratively.

07 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything
clearer.

How do I get an account?+

Registration requires a valid invitation linked to your email and company. Onboarding is managed by an administrator or an authorized employer.

Do I need an internet connection?+

Yes. Current flows retrieve and send data to the backend. Offline mode and automatic synchronization are not advertised.

How does location work?+

You can capture coordinates with device permissions or enter the report location manually.

Which devices are supported?+

The current app is native Android, for Android 8.0 or later. This release does not include an iOS app.

Can I download a public APK yet?+

Public APK download is being prepared. This page will link to the verified file once it is published; Google Play distribution is not enabled.
