# Asset provenance

- All 18 original repository assets, including `logo.png`, icons, legacy photos and `video2.mp4`, were preserved byte-for-byte. Retention does not create a new third-party license; existing rights and attribution remain applicable. Unused legacy assets are retained for recovery.
- `screens/incidents.png` and `screens/notifications.png` were extracted from the owner-provided `SafeWork-landing-preview.html`. They depict the app with synthetic test data. They are reference screenshots, not newly captured Render validation evidence. The profile capture was excluded to avoid showing an account email.
- `brand-mark.svg` and inline shield marks are code-native adaptations of the supplied design reference.
- Montserrat and Raleway variable fonts were reused from the existing mobile project's licensed resources. Their SIL Open Font License notices are preserved alongside the files: `fonts/montserrat-OFL.txt` and `fonts/raleway-OFL.txt`. Fonts are served locally without a third-party font request.
- The existing product video remains a project reference. Its presence does not establish that every feature shown in that earlier video is currently implemented. The team-video slot is explicitly pending; no missing `video1.mp4` URL is used.

The standalone HTML preview is not published or used as a replacement page. Its design was adapted into maintainable HTML, CSS, JavaScript and bilingual resource files.
